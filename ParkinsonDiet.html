#Example Opening Screen PARKINSON'S DYSAUTONOMIA Diet Specification Plant-Based Meal Plan | June 5 – 30, 2026 Budget: $100 | 26 Days | Vegan | Dairy-Free | Meat-Free Prepared for personal use by individual with Parkinson's Dysautonomia, age 61 Version 1.0 | June 4, 2026 Table of Contents 1. Overview & Purpose This document is a complete dietary requirements specification for a 26-day plant-based meal plan covering June 5 through June 30, 2026. It is designed for a 61-year-old individual living with Parkinson's Disease and Dysautonomia, working within a $100 grocery budget. The plan is 100% vegan — free of all meat and dairy — and is crafted to support neurological health, autonomic nervous system function, gut motility, and anti-inflammatory nutrition requirements specific to Parkinson's Dysautonomia. 1.1 Dietary Constraints No meat of any kind (beef, poultry, pork, seafood, or by-products) No dairy of any kind (milk, cheese, butter, yogurt, whey, casein) No eggs Low sodium where possible (supports blood pressure management) Whole food, minimally processed ingredients Orthostatic hypotension: eat smaller, more frequent meals; stay well hydrated Dopamine support: include lentils, peas, and fava beans (natural L-dopa precursors) Gut health: high fiber, fermented foods (miso), and consistent hydration Neuroinflammation: berries, flaxseed, walnuts, turmeric, ginger, and olive oil throughout Medication timing: high-protein meals may compete with levodopa absorption Consult your neurologist regarding meal and medication timing
